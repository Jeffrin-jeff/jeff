Introduction

What is LabLens?

A patient-friendly tool that reads your lab values, compares them with the right range for your age and sex, and explains what they may mean, without medical jargon.

Upload or Type

PDF, photo or manual entry, whichever is easiest for you.

Visual Range Bars

Private by Design
